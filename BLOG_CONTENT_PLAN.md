# Blog content plan

Editorial clusters (accessibility SEO)—**no fabricated traffic or ranking metrics**.

## Clusters

1. **Website accessibility** — automated checks, manual testing, remediation handoff  
2. **WCAG & standards literacy** — plain-language criteria, not legal advice  
3. **Alt text & non-text content** — social and web  
4. **Color contrast** — measurement and design workflow  
5. **Keyboard & focus** — quick checks before launch  
6. **Forms & inputs** — labels, errors, announcements  
7. **Video & captions** — captions, transcripts, player basics  
8. **Social media accessibility** — captions, hashtags, platform tasks  

## First 10 priority topics

1. How to write alt text for social images  
2. WCAG color contrast basics for content teams  
3. Keyboard accessibility quick check before launch  
4. Accessible link text patterns  
5. Heading structure for scannability and screen readers  
6. Form labels and error messages that help everyone  
7. Video captions vs transcripts—what to publish  
8. Social hashtag segmentation and readability  
9. Manual accessibility testing with free tools  
10. How to read an automated scan without over-claiming compliance
